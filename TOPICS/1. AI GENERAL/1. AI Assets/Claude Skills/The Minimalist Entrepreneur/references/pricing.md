# Pricing

## Purpose

Define and optimize pricing strategy based on value, cost, and real customer behavior.

---

## When to Apply

- Setting initial price
- Adjusting pricing strategy
- Unsure how to monetize
- Evaluating profitability

---

## Core Principle

Always charge.

Price is the primary signal of:
- value perception
- demand validation
- business viability

Free usage provides no reliable signal.

---

## Pricing Models

### 1. Cost-Based Pricing

- Price = cost + margin
- Margin typically 20–50%

Use when:
- Costs are clear and predictable
- Physical products or labor-heavy services

---

### 2. Value-Based Pricing

- Price based on perceived value to customer
- Independent of production cost

Use when:
- Digital products or software
- High impact relative to cost

---

### 3. Hybrid Approach

- Start with cost-based floor
- Adjust toward value-based ceiling

---

## Pricing Principles

- Start lower, increase over time
- Treat pricing as iterative (not fixed)
- Avoid default free usage
- Introduce tiers as product matures
- Separate pricing from marketing tactics

---

## Pricing Structure Evolution

Early stage:
- Single simple price

Later stage:
- Tiered pricing based on:
  - usage
  - features
  - customer segment

---

## Initial Pricing Workflow

Define:

- Cost per customer (time, tools, infrastructure)
- Alternative solutions and their pricing
- “No-brainer” price point for target user
- Minimum viable price for profitability

---

## Financial Model

Calculate:

- Monthly income target
- Required number of customers at current price
- Customer acquisition rate needed to reach target

Example logic:

- Revenue target / price per customer = required customers
- Customers / acquisition rate = time to target

---

## Constraints

- No free default offering
- No complex pricing at early stage
- No optimization before real usage data
- No dependency on scale assumptions

---

## Output

Define clearly:

1. Pricing model (cost-based, value-based, or hybrid)
2. Initial price point with reasoning
3. Future tier structure (if applicable)
4. Required number of customers to reach target income
5. Conditions to adjust or increase price
