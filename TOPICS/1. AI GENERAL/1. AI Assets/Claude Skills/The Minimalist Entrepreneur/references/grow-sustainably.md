# Grow Sustainably

## Purpose

Evaluate business decisions to ensure sustainable, profitable growth without unnecessary risk or burnout.

---

## When to Apply

- Making decisions about spending, hiring, or scaling
- Considering fundraising
- Evaluating cost structure
- Concerned about long-term sustainability

---

## Core Principle

Profitability ensures control and longevity.

Sustainability requires:
- revenue ≥ cost
- controlled growth pace
- efficient resource allocation

---

## Financial Model

### Profit Equation

Profit = Revenue − Costs

- Positive → sustainable
- Negative → limited runway

---

### Cost Structure

#### Variable Costs

- Increase with usage or sales
- Examples: payment fees, infrastructure, service delivery

---

#### Fixed Costs

- Independent of revenue
- Examples: salaries, tools, subscriptions

Primary risk:
- fixed costs growing faster than revenue

---

## Cost Management

Guidelines:

- Minimize fixed costs in early stages
- Delay hiring as long as possible
- Prefer tools and automation over manual labor
- Use freelancers before full-time employees
- Avoid long-term financial commitments

---

## Growth Strategy

- Do not optimize for speed alone
- Growth rate should follow demand, not ambition
- Small, consistent growth is acceptable and sustainable
- Avoid unnecessary expansion or market domination goals

---

## Decision Framework

For any decision, evaluate:

1. Impact on profitability  
   → Does it increase revenue or reduce cost?

2. Reversibility  
   → Can it be undone easily?

3. Customer-driven vs ego-driven  
   → Is it solving a real user need?

4. Simpler alternative  
   → Is there a lower-cost or lower-risk option?

5. Survival impact  
   → Does this move the business toward “default alive” or “default dead”?

---

## Fundraising Guidelines

- Prioritize self-sustaining revenue
- Consider external funding only if:
  - it accelerates validated growth
  - it does not create dependency
- Maintain control over decision-making

---

## Personal Sustainability

Avoid:

- overwork without leverage
- dependency on constant effort
- unstable workload patterns

Guidelines:

- scale workload gradually
- hire only when necessary
- maintain operational balance

---

## Constraints

- No spending without clear return
- No hiring without sustained workload
- No scaling without stable revenue
- No irreversible commitments early

---

## Output

Evaluate decision with:

1. Revenue impact
2. Cost impact
3. Risk level (reversible / irreversible)
4. Alignment with customer needs
5. Simpler alternative (if exists)
6. Sustainability outcome (alive vs dead trajectory)
