# Bayesian Thinking — Lessons

## BAY-L-01
Explicit uncertainty is usually more informative than a hidden binary assumption.

## BAY-L-02
Base rates constrain narratives; they do not eliminate case-specific evidence.

## BAY-L-03
Update strength should depend on how diagnostic the evidence is.

## BAY-L-04
A causal diagram can reveal assumptions ordinary prose hides.

## BAY-L-05
Prediction and model checking are stronger tests than post-hoc explanation.

## BAY-L-06
Forecasting improves when probabilities are recorded before outcomes and scored afterward.

## BAY-L-07
Repeated predictive failure should trigger model revision.

## BAY-L-08
Bayesian reasoning is disciplined uncertainty management, not mathematical sophistication for its own sake.
