# Negotiation — Concepts

Sources: Book 12 *Getting to Yes*; Book 13 *Never Split the Difference*.

## Principled foundation
Separate people from problems, positions from interests, and negotiation from willpower contests. BATNA defines the alternative to agreement. Objective criteria reduce arbitrary positional pressure. Options for mutual gain expand value before division.

## Tactical layer
Never Split the Difference adds tactical empathy, mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions, strategic use of “no”, execution confirmation and attention to hidden information/black swans.

## Information and leverage
Parties may have different interests, constraints, alternatives and beliefs. The quality of alternatives affects leverage. Questions and listening can surface constraints without requiring direct disclosure.

## Agreement and execution
A verbal agreement is not necessarily an executed outcome. Responsibilities, timing, dependencies and observable completion evidence reduce ambiguity.

## Boundary
Principled and tactical negotiation are complementary. Tactical techniques should improve information and communication, not replace BATNA, interests and objective standards.
