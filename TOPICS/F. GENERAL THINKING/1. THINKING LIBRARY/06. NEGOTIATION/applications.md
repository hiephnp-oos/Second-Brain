# Negotiation — Applications

## NEG-APP-01 — Supplier commercial negotiation
Establish alternatives, minimum acceptable outcome and objective market criteria before discussing price.

## NEG-APP-02 — Scope clarification
Translate disputed quantities or requirements from positions into interests and evidence.

## NEG-APP-03 — Internal resource negotiation
Identify real constraints and create options that trade on different priorities.

## NEG-APP-04 — Difficult counterpart
Use tactical empathy and calibrated questions to surface information, then return to BATNA and criteria.

## NEG-APP-05 — Contract execution
Treat agreement as incomplete until responsibilities, dates, dependencies and acceptance evidence are explicit.

## NEG-APP-06 — Hidden constraint discovery
When a proposal appears irrational, search for information that could make it rational from the counterpart's perspective.
