# Negotiation — Lessons

## NEG-L-01
The best negotiation often starts before the negotiation by improving alternatives.

## NEG-L-02
Positions are visible; interests and constraints explain them.

## NEG-L-03
Creating value can be more productive than arguing over existing value.

## NEG-L-04
Tactical communication is useful when it improves information, safety or execution.

## NEG-L-05
Objective criteria reduce arbitrary bargaining power.

## NEG-L-06
A refusal can be information about constraints.

## NEG-L-07
An agreement that cannot be executed is not a successful negotiation.

## NEG-L-08
Unexpected behavior is a reason to search for missing information before assuming irrationality.
