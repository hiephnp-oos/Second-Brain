# Negotiation — Mental Models

## NEG-MM-01 — Improve the walk-away
A better alternative can create more leverage than a stronger argument.

## NEG-MM-02 — Translate positions into interests
Ask what need, constraint or outcome makes the position valuable.

## NEG-MM-03 — Create value before dividing value
Look for differences in priorities, timing, risk and capabilities.

## NEG-MM-04 — Use objective standards
External criteria reduce bargaining to evidence instead of preference.

## NEG-MM-05 — Separate people from the problem
Manage relationship and emotion without obscuring substance.

## NEG-MM-06 — Tactical empathy is understanding, not agreement
Show understanding without conceding the claim.

## NEG-MM-07 — “No” can reveal boundaries
A refusal can provide information about constraints.

## NEG-MM-08 — Calibrated questions transfer problem-solving pressure
Ask questions that require the counterpart to work through constraints.

## NEG-MM-09 — Confirm execution
Specify who does what, by when, and how completion is verified.

## NEG-MM-10 — Search for hidden information
Unexpected constraints can make apparently irrational behavior rational.

## NEG-MM-11 — Do not confuse fairness with equality
Equal division is not automatically justified.

## NEG-MM-12 — Relationship preservation is not concession
A good relationship does not require accepting an inferior substantive deal.
