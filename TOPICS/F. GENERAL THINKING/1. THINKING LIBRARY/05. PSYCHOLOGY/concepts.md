# Psychology — Concepts

Source: Book 11 *Influence* — Robert B. Cialdini.

## Influence mechanisms
Reciprocity; commitment and consistency; social proof; liking; authority; scarcity; contrast/concession.

These mechanisms can trigger learned shortcuts that reduce cognitive effort but can also be exploited.

## Defensive recognition
Recognizing an influence cue is not the same as rejecting the proposal. Separate the mechanism from substantive merits.

## Boundary
These are behavioral mechanisms, not deterministic laws or a clinical diagnostic system. Context, incentives, culture and evidence matter.

## Practical synthesis
Identify cue → remove it mentally → evaluate offer/claim → verify authority, scarcity and social proof → decide independently.
