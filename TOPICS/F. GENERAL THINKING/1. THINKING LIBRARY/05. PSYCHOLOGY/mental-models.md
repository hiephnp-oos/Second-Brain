# Psychology — Mental Models

## PSY-MM-01 — Identify the shortcut
Name the social mechanism before accepting the resulting pressure.

## PSY-MM-02 — Remove the cue mentally
Ask whether the proposal remains attractive without authority, scarcity, reciprocity or social proof.

## PSY-MM-03 — Authority must be relevant
Status is not evidence of correctness on every subject.

## PSY-MM-04 — Test whether scarcity is real
Limited availability can inform, but artificial scarcity can manufacture urgency.

## PSY-MM-05 — Reassess commitments when circumstances change
Consistency is useful until underlying evidence or choice changes.

## PSY-MM-06 — Separate liking from proposal quality
A pleasant person can make a poor proposal.

## PSY-MM-07 — Treat social proof as selected evidence
Ask who is represented and who is missing.

## PSY-MM-08 — Reciprocity does not create obligation
A favor does not remove independent evaluation.

## PSY-MM-09 — Contrast can distort magnitude
Evaluate absolute value, not only relative size.

## PSY-MM-10 — Influence should preserve informed choice
Use persuasion to improve understanding rather than bypass judgment.
