# Psychology — Lessons

## PSY-L-01
An influence mechanism explains why a request feels compelling; it does not establish that the request is good.

## PSY-L-02
Separate the cue from the substance.

## PSY-L-03
Authority is strongest when relevant to the actual question.

## PSY-L-04
Social proof is evidence with selection effects.

## PSY-L-05
Consistency is useful until new evidence changes the underlying decision.

## PSY-L-06
Scarcity and urgency should be verified.

## PSY-L-07
Ethical influence preserves informed choice.
