# Systems Thinking — Concepts

Source: *Thinking in Systems*.

- System structure: elements, interconnections and purpose generate behavior.
- Stocks accumulate; flows change stocks.
- Balancing feedback counteracts change; reinforcing feedback amplifies it.
- Delays separate action from consequence and can create oscillation or overshoot.
- Nonlinearity makes proportional intuition unreliable.
- Information flows, rules and goals are structural variables.
- Resilience and self-organization can matter more than local optimization.
- Leverage can lie in information, rules, feedback, goals and paradigms; the hierarchy is a heuristic.
