# Systems Thinking — Lessons

## SYS-L-01
Repeated symptoms are evidence that structure deserves inspection.

## SYS-L-02
A stock cannot change instantly; the history of flows matters.

## SYS-L-03
Delayed feedback can make a sensible policy look wrong and a harmful policy look successful temporarily.

## SYS-L-04
Metrics and information flows are part of the system, not neutral observations of it.

## SYS-L-05
If an intervention repeatedly produces resistance, model the counteracting loop before increasing intervention strength.

## SYS-L-06
Local optimization can create system-level deterioration when components are tightly coupled.

## SYS-L-07
Resilience and adaptability can be more valuable than maximizing performance under one assumed condition.

## SYS-L-08
The highest-leverage intervention is not always the most feasible; leverage must be evaluated against constraints and side effects.
