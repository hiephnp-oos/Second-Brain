# Strategic Thinking — Mental Models

## STR-MM-01 — Diagnose before setting initiatives
Identify the critical obstacle before generating an action list.

## STR-MM-02 — Distinguish ambition from strategy
A desired outcome is not a strategy without a coherent approach.

## STR-MM-03 — Find leverage
Look for a focused intervention with disproportionate effect.

## STR-MM-04 — Concentrate resources
Strategy requires choice and exclusion.

## STR-MM-05 — Require action coherence
Actions should reinforce the guiding policy.

## STR-MM-06 — Exploit asymmetry
Ask what capability, position, information, timing or constraint differs in a usable way.

## STR-MM-07 — Use proximate objectives
When the end state is ambiguous, choose a feasible intermediate objective.

## STR-MM-08 — Look for organizational inertia
Persistent routines can be capabilities and constraints.

## STR-MM-09 — Strategy is choice under constraint
A strategy is meaningful partly because it excludes alternatives.

## STR-MM-10 — Diagnose the bottleneck
If many initiatives are active but progress remains weak, identify the constraint.

## STR-MM-11 — Coordinate actions around diagnosis
Strong strategy makes separate actions mutually reinforcing.

## STR-MM-12 — Test structural effect
If the environment adapts around an intervention, changing underlying conditions may matter more than increasing effort.
