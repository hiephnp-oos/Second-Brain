# Strategic Thinking — Concepts

- Strategy kernel: diagnosis + guiding policy + coherent action.
- Diagnosis simplifies around the critical obstacle.
- Guiding policy chooses the approach; it is not a list of goals.
- Coherent actions reinforce the policy.
- Proximate objectives make ambiguous challenges actionable.
- Leverage creates disproportionate effect through focused change.
- Design coordinates choices to overcome a challenge.
- Advantage exploits asymmetries rather than generic superiority.
- Focus creates power and requires exclusion.
- Inertia/dynamics explain persistence of obsolete routines and positions.
