# Strategic Thinking — Lessons

## STR-L-01
A list of goals is not a strategy.

## STR-L-02
Good strategy simplifies the challenge around a critical obstacle.

## STR-L-03
Focus makes limited resources coherent.

## STR-L-04
Actions should reinforce the guiding policy.

## STR-L-05
Leverage comes from concentration and asymmetry, not simply harder work.

## STR-L-06
A strategy that ignores organizational inertia can fail even when its logic is sound.

## STR-L-07
A proximate objective can create a path through ambiguity.

## STR-L-08
Strategic quality depends on diagnosis, coherence and leverage, not the impressiveness of ambition.
