# Critical Thinking — Reusable Lessons

- CR-L-01: accuracy and identity should be separable.
- CR-L-02: intelligence does not remove motivated reasoning.
- CR-L-03: salience is not probability.
- CR-L-04: confidence is not evidence.
- CR-L-05: outside views constrain inside stories.
- CR-L-06: deliberation is scarce; reserve it for unreliable intuition.
