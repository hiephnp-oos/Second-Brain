# Critical Thinking — Applications

## CR-APP-01 — Postmortem
Reconstruct what was knowable before the outcome. Separate decision quality from result.

## CR-APP-02 — Technical review
State claim, evidence, assumptions and falsification conditions. Check whether evidence discriminates among alternatives.

## CR-APP-03 — Management proposal
Reframe neutrally, inspect base rate, identify anchor and ask what evidence would reverse the recommendation.

## CR-APP-04 — Vendor comparison
Separate memorable features from decision-relevant evidence and use equivalent criteria.

## CR-APP-05 — Personal belief update
Record current belief, confidence, evidence for/against and the observation that would materially change it.

## CR-APP-06 — Persuasion defense
Identify influence cues, then reassess the underlying claim independently.
