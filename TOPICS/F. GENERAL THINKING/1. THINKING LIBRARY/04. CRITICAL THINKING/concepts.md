# Critical Thinking — Concepts

- Scout mindset: beliefs are models to improve, not identities to defend.
- Motivated reasoning can protect identity or desired conclusions.
- System 1/System 2 is a useful model of fast vs effortful judgment, not literal brain architecture.
- Anchoring can pull estimates toward initial references.
- Availability can overweight memorable events.
- Representativeness can underweight base rates.
- Framing can change preferences over equivalent outcomes.
- Loss aversion can magnify psychological weight of losses.
- Regression to the mean can mimic causal improvement.
- Confidence and narrative coherence can exceed evidential support.
