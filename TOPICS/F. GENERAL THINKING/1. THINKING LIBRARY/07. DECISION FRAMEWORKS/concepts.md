# Decision Frameworks — Concepts

- Decision quality is distinct from outcome quality.
- **Resulting** judges a decision only from its outcome and creates hindsight distortion.
- Important beliefs benefit from explicit probabilities.
- Decision records preserve the original information state.
- Dissent can improve group accuracy when evidence-focused.
- Premortems expose failure paths before commitment.
- Backcasting works backward from a desired future.
- Precommitment protects against predictable future drift.
- Outcomes are feedback, not a complete score of decision quality.
