# Decision Frameworks — Concepts

Source: Book 14 *Thinking in Bets* — Annie Duke.

## Decision quality vs outcome quality
A good decision can produce a bad outcome and a bad decision can produce a good outcome. Resulting judges reasoning from the realized result and creates hindsight distortion.

## Probabilistic decisions
Important uncertain beliefs benefit from explicit probabilities. This makes disagreement inspectable and later calibration possible.

## Decision records
Record information, assumptions, alternatives and confidence at decision time to preserve the original information state.

## Dissent
Evidence-focused disagreement can expose missing evidence and alternative interpretations.

## Premortem, backcasting and precommitment
Premortem works backward from failure. Backcasting works backward from a desired future. Precommitment protects future behavior against predictable drift.

## Feedback
Outcomes are feedback, not a complete score. Review what was knowable at the time and distinguish model, evidence, execution and variance.
