# Decision Frameworks — Mental Models

## DEC-MM-01 — Judge from the information set available then
Do not import future knowledge into the original decision.

## DEC-MM-02 — Turn meaningful uncertainty into probabilities
A probability makes hidden uncertainty explicit.

## DEC-MM-03 — Keep a decision record
Record assumptions, alternatives, evidence and confidence.

## DEC-MM-04 — State what would change the bet
Define evidence or conditions that should move the decision.

## DEC-MM-05 — Invite informed dissent
Seek people who can expose missing evidence or failure modes.

## DEC-MM-06 — Run a premortem
Assume failure and identify plausible causes before execution.

## DEC-MM-07 — Backcast
Start from the desired future and identify required conditions/actions.

## DEC-MM-08 — Precommit against predictable drift
Set rules before predictable future pressure arrives.

## DEC-MM-09 — Score the forecast
Compare probabilities with outcomes to improve calibration.

## DEC-MM-10 — Separate decision and execution error
A sound decision can fail through execution; a poor decision can succeed through variance.

## DEC-MM-11 — Update the process
After an outcome, change the rule or assumption that caused the error rather than merely changing the conclusion.
