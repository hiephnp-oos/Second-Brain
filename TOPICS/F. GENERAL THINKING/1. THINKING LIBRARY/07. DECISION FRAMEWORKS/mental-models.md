# Decision Frameworks — Mental Models

- DEC-MM-01: evaluate the decision at the information set available then.
- DEC-MM-02: turn meaningful uncertainty into explicit probabilities.
- DEC-MM-03: keep a decision record.
- DEC-MM-04: state what would change the bet.
- DEC-MM-05: invite informed dissent.
- DEC-MM-06: run a premortem.
- DEC-MM-07: backcast from the desired future.
- DEC-MM-08: precommit against predictable drift.
- DEC-MM-09: close the loop with scoring.
