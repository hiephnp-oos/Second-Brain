# Decision Frameworks — Applications

- DEC-APP-01: major project → record alternatives, assumptions, probabilities, downside and disconfirming evidence.
- DEC-APP-02: postmortem → reconstruct what was knowable then before judging process.
- DEC-APP-03: team decision → require explicit assumptions/probabilities and dissent.
- DEC-APP-04: high-emotion decision → predefine thresholds or stop conditions.
- DEC-APP-05: forecast-to-decision → combine probability with action value, reversibility and downside.
