# Decision Frameworks — Applications

## DEC-APP-01 — Major project decision
Record alternatives, assumptions, probabilities, downside and reversibility before commitment.

## DEC-APP-02 — Career decision
Separate preference from forecast. Record probabilities for uncertain outcomes and evidence that would change the choice.

## DEC-APP-03 — Technical investment
Use premortem to identify failure modes and distinguish model uncertainty from execution risk.

## DEC-APP-04 — Vendor selection
Judge decision quality using information available at selection time rather than later performance.

## DEC-APP-05 — Postmortem
Compare predicted probabilities with outcomes and classify error as evidence/model/execution/variance.

## DEC-APP-06 — Recurring operational decision
Create a lightweight decision record and precommitment rule when pressure is predictable.
