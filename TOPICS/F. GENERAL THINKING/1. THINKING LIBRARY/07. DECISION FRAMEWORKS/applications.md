# Decision Frameworks — Applications

## DEC-APP-01 — Major project decision
Record alternatives, assumptions, probability ranges, downside, opportunity costs and reversibility before commitment. Add what evidence would change the choice, then review the original reasoning separately from the eventual result.

## DEC-APP-02 — Career decision
Separate preference from forecast. Record probabilities for uncertain outcomes and evidence that would change the choice. Include plausible branches such as staying, moving, delay or a worse-than-expected role; do not collapse the future to the outcome that later occurred.

## DEC-APP-03 — Technical investment
Use a premortem to identify failure modes and backcasting to identify conditions required for success. Map multiple plausible futures, state probability assumptions and distinguish model uncertainty from execution risk. Expected value is useful for comparison but cannot guarantee the outcome of a single investment.

## DEC-APP-04 — Vendor selection
Judge decision quality using information, alternatives, trade-offs and uncertainty available at selection time rather than later performance. Record why one vendor was chosen and what new evidence would change the ranking; separate supplier execution failure from an unreasonable original choice.

## DEC-APP-05 — Postmortem
Compare prior probabilities with actual outcomes across repeated cases. Examine evidence quality, model assumptions, execution, luck and external causes. Avoid crediting every success to skill or excusing every failure as bad luck.

## DEC-APP-06 — Recurring operational decision
Create a lightweight decision record and a precommitment rule when pressure or emotional reactions are predictable. Add a pause or other proportionate friction when “tilt” could drive a decision against long-term objectives.

## DEC-APP-07 — Decision group / design review
Define the group’s truth-seeking norms: accuracy over winning, relevant information sharing, diversity of viewpoints, accountability for evidence and exploration of competing explanations. Evaluate the claim rather than the seniority of the person proposing it; actively guard against consensus and confirmatory drift.

## DEC-APP-08 — Forecast with expected value
List outcomes, estimated probabilities, values and material opportunity costs. Calculate the probability-weighted expected value, test sensitivity to uncertain assumptions, and state clearly that the average guides repeated comparable decisions rather than predicting the result of one case.

## DEC-APP-09 — High-pressure or emotional choice
Recognize signals of tilt such as anger, regret, urgency or short-term reward seeking. Pause, re-read the decision rule and consider how a future self would assess the action. Treat the pause as error reduction, not a guarantee against bias.
