# Decision Frameworks — Lessons

## DEC-L-01
A bad outcome does not prove a bad decision; a good outcome does not prove a good decision.

## DEC-L-02
Decision records protect against memory rewriting.

## DEC-L-03
Probabilities make disagreement inspectable and learning possible.

## DEC-L-04
Premortems are most useful before commitment.

## DEC-L-05
Precommitment protects future decisions from predictable future incentives.

## DEC-L-06
Postmortem review should improve process rather than justify the past.

## DEC-L-07
Separate variance from model, evidence and execution error.

## DEC-L-08
Convert outcomes into calibrated feedback rather than emotional verdicts.
