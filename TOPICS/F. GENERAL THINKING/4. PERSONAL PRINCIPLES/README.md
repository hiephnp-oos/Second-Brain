# Personal Principles

Durable personal rules derived from repeated reflection and validated experience.

## Boundary
Personal Principles are user-owned rules for future behavior. They are not book summaries, framework definitions, or generic life advice.

## Promotion gate
Promote a principle only after a recurring pattern is observed in Reflection, the mechanism is understood, the rule materially changes future behavior, and scope/failure conditions are explicit.

No principle is created merely because a book recommends a behavior.
