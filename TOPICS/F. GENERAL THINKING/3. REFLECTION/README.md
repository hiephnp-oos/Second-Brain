# Reflection

Experience-derived learning from applying the General Thinking layer to real situations.

## Boundary
Reflection is not book summary and not framework knowledge. It records what happened when a thinking tool was used in practice.

A reflection should answer:
Situation → Framework used → Prediction / decision → Outcome → What was wrong or right → Updated lesson.

## Contract
A review may include situation/context, decision/action, framework/model, assumptions/uncertainty, expected outcome, observed outcome, root cause of the gap, reusable lesson, and candidate update.

Individual reviews live in REVIEWS/. Durable lessons extracted from multiple reviews may be promoted to LESSONS/.

Do not promote a one-off observation into a personal principle without repeated evidence.
