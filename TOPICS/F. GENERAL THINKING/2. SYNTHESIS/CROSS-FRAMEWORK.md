# Cross-Framework Patterns

## Strategic interaction + systems thinking
Game Theory contributes actor incentives, expectations, strategic responses, commitment, and equilibrium. Systems Thinking contributes feedback loops, delays, accumulated effects, information flows, rules, and leverage points.

Integrated pattern: when multiple actors repeatedly respond to each other inside a persistent system, model both strategic choices and feedback structure.

## Bayesian reasoning + forecasting
Bayesian Thinking contributes explicit uncertainty, priors, generative models, updating, and posterior prediction. Forecasting contributes reference classes, decomposition, calibration, scoring, and incremental revision.

Integrated pattern: start with a reference class and explicit prior, decompose the question, update probabilities as evidence arrives, and evaluate the forecast against outcomes.

## Critical thinking + Bayesian reasoning
Treat a claim as a hypothesis, identify evidence that would discriminate among explanations, assign uncertainty, and update rather than defend the original narrative.

## Decision frameworks + forecasting
Make the forecast explicit before acting, record assumptions and probabilities, precommit against predictable failure modes, then evaluate the decision using information available at the time.

## Negotiation + game theory
Treat negotiation as an interactive game while preserving the distinction between interests, alternatives, and stated positions.

## Strategic thinking + systems thinking
Diagnose the critical obstacle at the system level, then choose a focused policy and mutually reinforcing actions that alter the relevant structure.

## Psychology + critical thinking
Identify the psychological mechanism without assuming that the mechanism proves the claim or justifies the influence attempt.
