# Framework Index

| Framework | Use when | Core question |
|---|---|---|
| Game Theory | Other intelligent actors can react to your choices | What will they do next, and how does that change my best move? |
| Systems Thinking | Outcomes emerge from interacting parts and feedback | What structure is producing the observed behavior? |
| Bayesian Thinking | Evidence is incomplete and beliefs should update | How much should this evidence change my belief? |
| Critical Thinking | Claims, assumptions, or reasoning may be weak | What exactly is supported, and what would falsify it? |
| Psychology | Behavior, bias, motivation, or social influence matters | What human mechanism is driving the behavior? |
| Negotiation | Parties have partially aligned and conflicting interests | How can interests, alternatives, and commitments change the outcome? |
| Decision Theory | A choice must be made under uncertainty/trade-offs | Which action has the best decision-relevant value? |
| Strategic Thinking | Competitive or interactive choices shape outcomes | Where is the leverage, and what response will it induce? |

The index is intentionally compact. Detailed framework knowledge belongs under the Thinking Library when actual material is added.
