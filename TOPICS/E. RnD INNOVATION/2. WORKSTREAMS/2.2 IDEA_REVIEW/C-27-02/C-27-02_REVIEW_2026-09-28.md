## Verification

A full-scale 84-day comparative study of conventional, silver-embedded antimicrobial, and filter-based antimicrobial showerheads found no statistically significant differences in absolute abundance of targeted drinking-water-associated pathogens between head types. The tested filter-based head also showed no significant chlorine reduction despite its marketed claim. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12993570/

Other studies describe time-dependent microbial/community behavior and variation by organism, water/aerosol phase, and showerhead age. Sources: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiomes/articles/10.3389/frmbi.2023.1292571/full ; https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiomes/articles/10.3389/frmbi.2024.1416055/full ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0043135425009364

Commercial filter showerheads advertise replaceable 0.08 µm membranes and bacterial-reduction performance under stated laboratory conditions. These vendor claims establish market precedent, not independent real-use efficacy. Sources: https://www.mwt-us.com/skystream ; https://www.mwt-nl.com/pg360-ifu

## Synthesis and evaluation

The problem is relevant, but “localized terminal-outlet treatment” is not a defined mechanism or architecture. Generic antimicrobial and filter showerheads already exist. The comparative study cautions that material/marketing claims may not translate into reduced pathogen abundance under realistic operation. The evidence does not prove all filtration or antimicrobial mechanisms ineffective; efficacy is mechanism-, organism-, endpoint-, and time-dependent.

The candidate lacks a specified mechanism, target organisms, pressure/flow envelope, service life, replacement interval, aerosol endpoint, material compatibility, safety/compliance boundary, and measurable delta versus existing products. Added complexity is not justified by a demonstrated benefit.

## Disposition

DROP as currently framed as a standalone innovation candidate. This does not mean every antimicrobial/filter solution is technically impossible. The current candidate has no defined differentiated mechanism, demonstrated efficacy, or explicit outcome delta. Reopen only with a specific mechanism and measurable differentiated outcome.
