# Integrated Diverter Cartridge (OHM) — One-Pager
**Date:** 2026-09-30

## Insight – Consumer Problem Solved – Problem Statement

- Bath-shower mixers typically have two separate controls: a mixing cartridge for temperature adjustment and a diverter valve for outlet selection.
- **Opportunity:** Integrate both functions into one cartridge to simplify bath-shower operation through a unified control interface.

## Proposed Solution – Concept / Technology Description

- Develop an integrated 2-stage diverter cartridge combining hot/cold mixing and outlet selection in one cartridge.
- One handle controls two functions:
  - Left / Right: Hot–Cold temperature mixing.
  - Neutral (0°): OFF.
  - Lift Up (+15°): Handshower.
  - Lower Down (−15°): Bath Spout.
- **Basic Architecture:** 2 Inlets: Hot + Cold; 2 Outlets: Handshower + Spout.
- Integrate mixing and diverter functions into a single control module.

## Competitive Landscape

- Regular OHM architecture typically uses a mixing cartridge (2 inlets / 1 outlet) with a separate diverter mechanism.

## Benefits & Solution Justification

- **Simplified Operation:** One handle controls both temperature and outlet selection, making bath-shower operation more convenient.
- **Product Integration:** Potential to reduce component count and simplify product assembly.

## Unknowns & High Risk Areas

- Integrated cartridge feasibility, flow performance, outlet isolation and durability.
