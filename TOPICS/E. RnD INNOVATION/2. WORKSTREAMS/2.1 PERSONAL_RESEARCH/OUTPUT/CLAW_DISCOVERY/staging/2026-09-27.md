### MP-01 — Shower water-efficiency vs aerosol / hygiene trade-off
**Strategic/Tier alignment:** Tier 1 — Showering Innovation & Technology / Wellness + Circularity.
**Status:** EVIDENCED technical/system-level tension; user demand for a specific product response remains UNKNOWN.

- WHO: users / building operators seeking water-efficient showering; health-sensitive environments are a relevant application context.
- CONTEXT: EPA WaterSense treats spray force, spray coverage and pressure compensation as core shower performance attributes. Recent 2026 research also highlights a potential trade-off between water conservation, aerosol generation and microbial water quality in shower systems.
- NEED / PAIN: maintain acceptable shower performance and water efficiency without creating an avoidable hygiene/aerosol downside.
- BEST EXISTING SOLUTION: low-flow / pressure-compensating / fluidic shower technologies, plus separate water-management and infection-control measures.
- WHAT IT SOLVES: efficient showering and acceptable spray experience.
- REMAINING LIMITATION: performance optimization is generally separated from terminal-outlet microbial/aerosol management; a fixture-side architecture that jointly addresses both is not established in the current Knowledge Sheet.
- MEANINGFUL DELTA: demonstrate a measurable shower architecture that preserves spray performance and water efficiency while reducing an identified aerosol or microbial-risk metric, without adding disproportionate maintenance or complexity.
- REQUIRED PRODUCT / PHYSICAL FUNCTION: control terminal water discharge and/or internal water retention in a way that can be measured against both shower-performance and hygiene/aerosol criteria.
- Candidate architectures: low-retention flow path; controlled discharge geometry; post-use purge/flush; combined low-aerosol outlet + cleanable internal path. These are **PROPOSED** only.
- User value: potentially safer / more controlled shower experience with maintained performance; not yet validated as a market demand.
- Fitting applicability: showerhead / handshower / hose / terminal outlet.
- Evidence status: EVIDENCED technical tension; market demand for a combined solution UNKNOWN.
- Main uncertainty: whether a fixture-side intervention can produce a meaningful measurable benefit without compromising spray feel, flow, noise, scale resistance, durability or maintenance.
- Discovery disposition: **WATCH**
