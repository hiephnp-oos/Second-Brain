# Minimalist Review

## Purpose

Evaluate any business decision, plan, or strategy using minimalist principles to ensure simplicity, profitability, and alignment with real demand.

---

## When to Apply

- Making a decision (build, hire, spend, scale, pivot)
- Comparing multiple options
- Unsure about direction
- Need a quick validation (“gut-check”)

---

## Core Principles

Evaluate decision against:

1. Community-first  
2. Manual before automation  
3. Minimal scope  
4. Sales before marketing  
5. Time before money  
6. Profitability focus  
7. Customer-driven growth  
8. Long-term alignment  

---

## Evaluation Framework

Assess using the following criteria:

### 1. Customer Alignment

- Does this solve a real problem?
- Is it based on actual user demand?

---

### 2. Simplicity

- Is this the simplest possible approach?
- Can scope be reduced further?

---

### 3. Validation

- Has anyone paid for this?
- Can it be tested manually?

---

### 4. Resource Efficiency

- Can this be done with time instead of money?
- Is cost justified by expected return?

---

### 5. Profitability Impact

- Does this move toward positive cash flow?
- Does it increase or reduce cost burden?

---

### 6. Reversibility

- Can this decision be undone easily?
- Does it create long-term constraints?

---

### 7. Demand-Driven Growth

- Are customers asking for this?
- Or is it based on assumptions?

---

### 8. Strategic Alignment

- Does this align with long-term direction?
- Is it something worth maintaining over time?

## Decision Outcomes

Based on evaluation:

- **Proceed**  
  → Strong alignment across criteria

- **Simplify**  
  → Overbuilt or unnecessary complexity

- **Delay**  
  → Insufficient validation or unclear demand

- **Reject / Pivot**  
  → Misaligned with core principles

---

## Constraints

- No decisions based on assumptions alone
- No complexity without validation
- No spending without clear return
- No scaling without demand

---

## Output

Provide:

1. Clear decision (proceed / simplify / delay / reject)
2. Simplest viable version of the plan
3. Primary risk to monitor
4. One immediate action to validate
