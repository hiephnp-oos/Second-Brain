# First Customers

## Purpose

Acquire the first 10–100 customers through direct, manual sales and iterative learning.

---

## When to Apply

- Already have MVP or manual solution
- Need initial paying customers
- No traction or low early adoption
- Preparing for product-market fit

---

## Core Principle

Prioritize **direct selling over launching**.

Early growth comes from:
- one-to-one interaction
- learning from each conversation
- iterative improvement

---

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Start from Existing Relationships

Target closest network first:

- Friends and family
- Existing contacts

Actions:

- Present product as a solution, not an idea
- Ask for purchase or real feedback
- Validate willingness to pay

---

### Step 2 — Expand to Community

Engage relevant users:

- People already discussing the problem
- Members of your identified community

Actions:

- List potential users (minimum 10–20)
- Reach out individually
- Demonstrate product or solution
- Collect direct feedback

---

### Step 3 — Cold Outreach

Scale outreach manually:

- Identify potential users outside network
- Contact via email, message, or call

Principles:

- Personalize each message
- Focus on the user’s current workflow/problem
- Offer clear value

Each interaction = data for improvement

---

## Sales Principles

- Selling = helping users solve real problems
- No persuasion without value
- Rejections provide signal:
  - wrong customer
  - unclear value
  - insufficient problem severity

---

## Pricing Strategy

- Always charge (avoid free usage)
- Initial options:
  - Cost-based (cost + margin)
  - Value-based (impact to customer)

Guidelines:

- Start with simple pricing
- Adjust based on feedback and demand
- Increase over time as value improves

---

## Metrics

Track:

- Number of conversations per week
- Conversion rate (contact → paying user)
- Revenue per customer
- Feedback frequency and patterns

Key signal:

- Repeat usage without prompting = early product-market fit

---

## Constraints

- No reliance on ads or viral growth
- No mass marketing before validation
- No scaling before consistent conversions

---

## Output

Define clearly:

1. List of 10 contacts to approach immediately
2. List of 10 community users to reach out to
3. Personalized outreach structure (not template spam)
4. Initial pricing approach
5. Weekly sales target and tracking method
