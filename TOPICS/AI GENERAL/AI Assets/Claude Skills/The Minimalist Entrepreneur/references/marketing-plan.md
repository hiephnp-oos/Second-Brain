# Marketing Plan

## Purpose

Build a scalable marketing system based on content and audience growth, not paid advertising.

---

## When to Apply

- Already have product-market fit (~100 customers)
- Need to scale acquisition
- Want to build audience through content
- Transitioning from direct sales to marketing

---

## Core Principle

Marketing = sales at scale.

Start with:
- time (content)
- consistency
- feedback loops

Delay spending money until:
- audience is defined
- messaging is proven

---

## Prerequisites

Ensure before applying:

- Active community connection
- Paying customers
- Experience with direct sales
- Clear understanding of customer problems

---

## Customer Journey

Every user moves through:

1. Engage — discover content
2. Follow — show interest
3. Research — explore product
4. Consider — evaluate value
5. Buy — convert

All stages must be supported

---

## Audience Strategy

- Community = initial base (high relevance)
- Audience = scalable reach (broader exposure)

Goal:
- Convert strangers → followers → customers

---

## Content Strategy

### Level 1 — Educate

- Share knowledge, insights, lessons
- Focus on practical value
- Build credibility

---

### Level 2 — Inspire

- Share journey, progress, challenges
- Build connection and trust

---

### Level 3 — Entertain

- Increase reach through engaging formats
- Simplify and amplify ideas

---

## Channel Strategy

- Select 1–2 primary platforms
- Maintain consistency over frequency
- Separate:
  - personal presence
  - business communication

---

## Execution Guidelines

- Share real work (build in public)
- Focus on ideas, not self-promotion
- Track engagement signals
- Double down on what works

---

## Email Strategy

- Build owned audience channel
- Collect emails early
- Offer clear value in exchange
- Use email for:
  - deeper content
  - conversion
  - retention

---

## Content System

Define:

- Platforms (1–2 primary)
- Posting schedule (consistent cadence)
- Content types mapped to:
  - educate
  - inspire
  - entertain

---

## Paid Marketing (Later Stage)

Only consider when:

- Organic traction exists
- Customer profile is clear
- Conversion funnel is stable

Constraints:

- Do not exceed customer value (CAC < LTV)
- Prioritize retention over acquisition spend

---

## Constraints

- No ads before validation
- No multi-channel overload
- No content without feedback loop
- No scaling without repeatable conversion

---

## Output

Define clearly:

1. Primary platform(s) and posting schedule
2. Content ideas across 3 levels (educate, inspire, entertain)
3. Email acquisition and usage strategy
4. “Build in public” content plan
5. Conditions for starting paid marketing
