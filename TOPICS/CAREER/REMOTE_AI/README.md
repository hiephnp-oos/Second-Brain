# Remote / AI

## Purpose / Scope

Routing and durable evaluation rules for remote, contract, part-time, freelance, and AI-enabled opportunities that can leverage the user's engineering background and AI capabilities.

## Current Context

This workstream runs in parallel with local/full-time Job Search. It is especially relevant when no suitable local 9–5 role is identified, but it may also surface higher-leverage opportunities regardless of local availability.

The target includes work that can combine manufacturing / engineering / quality knowledge with AI-assisted research, analysis, automation, documentation, workflow design, data work, or other practical AI capabilities. Evening compatibility matters for opportunities intended to be performed alongside the current 9–5 role.

## Active Artifacts / References

- `../CAREER_PROFILE.md` — canonical career baseline.
- External search/tracking tools may hold individual remote opportunities, engagement terms, application status, and compensation details.
- Durable remote-work constraints and reusable fit rules belong in this README or the shared profile.

## Working Rules

- Do not evaluate Remote / AI opportunities using traditional full-time ATS match alone.
- Consider the combined value of engineering domain knowledge, quality/manufacturing experience, AI capability, deliverable fit, remote feasibility, engagement type, and schedule compatibility.
- Distinguish true remote work from hybrid, location-restricted, timezone-restricted, or contractor arrangements.
- For evening-compatible work, verify expected hours, synchronous meeting load, timezone requirements, and availability expectations where evidence is available.
- Separate skill gaps that can realistically be bridged from hard requirements that materially block the opportunity.
- Do not inflate AI capability or claim production experience that is not established.
- Contract, freelance, and part-time work should be evaluated on scope, deliverables, time commitment, compensation, and repeatability, not just job title.

## Decisions / Status

- This workstream is active and operates in parallel with Job Search and Company Radar.
- It is intentionally not a separate job database.
- A future database may be introduced when remote opportunity volume and query/history requirements create a demonstrated limitation in the current external-tracker model.

## Routing

Use this workstream for remote jobs, AI-adjacent engineering work, contract/freelance work, part-time work, and opportunities intended to fit around an existing 9–5 schedule.

For local/full-time roles route to `../JOB_SEARCH/README.md`. For company expansion and regional opportunity signals route to `../COMPANY_RADAR/README.md`.
