# Existing Ideas

## Purpose

Maintain and analyze existing R&D ideas, verify their evidence, identify gaps, compare with current Knowledge Sheet knowledge, improve/synthesize ideas, and prepare targeted research requests.

## Working Rules

- Treat the idea record as the starting hypothesis, not established fact.
- Check relevant Knowledge Sheet records first.
- Verify important commercial, technical, mechanism, material, supplier, and patent claims.
- Separate supported facts, inference, assumptions, and proposed improvements.
- Identify evidence gaps that can change the decision.
- Do not force dead-end ideas into POC or Deep Analyze.
- Create a Knowledge Candidate when a reusable finding is discovered.

## Tool Use

Team-facing work uses NotebookLM + Custom Gemini + released `Prompt.csv`.

When deeper external research is required, the maintainer uses Personal Research and returns a concise verified result.

## Prompt

Use `Prompt.csv` in this folder.

## Output

Idea records and review material remain here. Reusable knowledge is proposed for Knowledge Sheet review rather than silently copied.
