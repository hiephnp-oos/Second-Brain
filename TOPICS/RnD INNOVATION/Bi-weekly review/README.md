# Bi-weekly Review

## Purpose

Recurring review of existing ideas, evidence gaps, research requests, decisions, follow-ups, and reusable knowledge candidates.

## Working Rules

- Review current ideas against the latest available Knowledge Sheet context.
- Identify the few evidence gaps that materially affect decisions.
- Distinguish verification needs from new idea generation.
- Convert important unresolved questions into targeted research requests.
- Identify reusable findings as Knowledge Candidates.
- Do not automatically change authoritative Knowledge Sheet or idea records from a review.

## Tool Use

Team-facing work uses NotebookLM + Custom Gemini + released `Prompt.csv`.

External research is performed by the maintainer through Personal Research when requested or required.

## Prompt

Use `Prompt.csv` in this folder.

## Output

Each review should leave a concise record of:
- items reviewed
- important findings
- evidence gaps
- research requests
- decisions/follow-ups
- Knowledge Candidates
