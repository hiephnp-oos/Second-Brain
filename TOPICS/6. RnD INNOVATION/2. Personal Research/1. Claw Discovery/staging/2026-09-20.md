## RS-10 — Market Pull Scout

### MP-01 — Recurring showerhead clogging / maintenance burden
**Status:** EVIDENCED signal; candidate mechanisms remain PROPOSED.

- WHO: shower users in hard-water / mineral-deposit environments.
- CONTEXT: shower spray performance degrades as mineral deposits accumulate on nozzles and wetted surfaces.
- NEED / PAIN: maintain spray performance without frequent manual rubbing, disassembly, soaking, or chemical cleaning.
- BEST EXISTING SOLUTION: flexible rubber/silicone rub-clean nozzles, removable spray faces, periodic descaling.
- WHAT IT SOLVES: surface deposits can be removed manually and spray performance can be restored.
- REMAINING LIMITATION: maintenance still depends on user intervention; recurring hard-water deposits remain a repeated problem.
- MEANINGFUL DELTA: reduce the need for manual maintenance by using passive/flow-driven or other automatic deposit-disruption/drainage behavior.
- REQUIRED FUNCTION: maintain or restore nozzle openness / drainage without routine user disassembly.
- Evidence: Reddit user reports of recurrent clogging; Glow shower maintenance guidance; current commercial products using rub-clean/self-cleaning nozzles.
- External evidence: https://www.reddit.com/r/Plumbing/comments/1ps61cx/shower_head_keeps_getting_clogged/ ; https://www.reddit.com/r/HomeMaintenance/comments/1ajti4d/excess_mineral_buildup_on_shower_head/ ; https://www.showerstoyou.co.uk/uploads/documents/Glow_Thermostatic_A5_handbook_installation_and_user_guide.pdf ; https://www.lowes.com/pd/Baginizer-NSF-Certified-Filtered-Handheld-Shower-Head-For-Hard-Water-9-Spray-Modes-High-Pressure-Self-Cleaning-Nozzles/5022568256
- Existing Knowledge: TR-001 pressure-compensating/self-flushing emitter is an active enabler for stable flow/self-flushing concepts; TR-005 directional wettability is an active enabler for passive water transport; TR-046 piezo/ultrasonic fouling cleaning is a transfer signal.

### MP-02 — Magnetic handshower docking reliability
**Status:** EVIDENCED signal; proposed architecture is PROPOSED.

- WHO: users of magnetic handshowers, especially where the handshower is repeatedly docked/removed or the hose/handshower can impose impact loads.
- CONTEXT: magnetic docking is now a mature commercial interface, but recent user reports include cracked brackets/connectors and failures after months of use.
- NEED / PAIN: retain the convenience of one-handed magnetic docking without premature structural failure, leakage, or impact damage.
- BEST EXISTING SOLUTION: magnetic docking systems such as Moen Magnetix and Delta SureDock.
- WHAT IT SOLVES: easy release and secure re-docking.
- REMAINING LIMITATION: evidence from current customer reviews indicates failures of plastic brackets/connectors and impact-related damage; this is a reliability gap rather than a lack of docking availability.
- MEANINGFUL DELTA: separate magnetic alignment/retention function from the primary structural load path, with controlled impact/load transfer and replaceable wear element where practical.
- REQUIRED FUNCTION: dock automatically while limiting peak mechanical load transferred into fragile wetted/structural components.
- Evidence: current 2026 Lowes/Walmart customer reports; manufacturer/distributor descriptions of magnetic docking.
- External evidence: https://www.lowes.com/pd/Moen-Magnetix-Engage-Chrome-6-Spray-Handheld-Shower/1000209883 ; https://www.lowes.com/pd/Moen-Engage-Matte-Black-EcoPerformance-35Inch-Handheld-Showerhead-with-Magnetic-Docking-System/5018730951 ; https://www.deltafaucet.com/bathroom/product/75609BL.html ; https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/735537416
- Existing Knowledge: CT-013 Moen Magnetix; ST-017 magnetic docking spray head; TR-050 Magnetic Docking / Auto-Align Shower Attachment Interface. Magnetic docking itself is BASELINE/commercially common.

### TP-01 — Fluid-driven rotating cleaning mechanism
**Status:** EVIDENCED technology; shower transfer is UNKNOWN/WATCH.

- Source application: industrial CIP / pressure-cleaning nozzles.
- Function: use reaction force or turbine drive from flowing liquid to rotate a spray element without external power.
- Physical principle: jet reaction / hydraulic turbine torque.
- Demonstrated capability: commercial rotating cleaning nozzles operate over controlled pressure/flow ranges and use rotation to improve cleaning coverage.
- Transfer boundary: industrial examples generally use higher flow and/or pressure than domestic shower systems; bearing/seal durability, scale tolerance, noise, pressure loss and potable-water materials must be verified.
- Potential fitting insertion: spray face / internal maintenance module.
- Evidence: Spraying Systems TankJet D41800E; CYCO 36500 self-rotating PTFE spray head.
- External evidence: https://www.spray.com/products/tank-cleaning/tankjet-d41800e ; https://www.cyconozzles.com/product/36500-self-rotating-ptfe-spray-head/
- Product capability proposed: periodic low-power hydraulic rotation that mechanically sweeps/agitates a maintenance spray pattern.

### TP-02 — Ultrasonic anti-fouling / scale-control mechanism
**Status:** EVIDENCED technology; fitting transfer is UNKNOWN/WATCH.

- Source application: industrial ultrasonic fouling/scale control and precision cleaning.
- Function: high-frequency acoustic excitation can reduce fouling/cleaning deposits; ultrasonic cleaning commonly uses piezoelectric transducers and acoustic cavitation/agitation.
- Physical principle: high-frequency acoustic excitation; cavitation/agitation depending on operating regime.
- Demonstrated capability: commercial systems are used for cleaning and industrial fouling/scale management.
- Transfer boundary: domestic shower integration requires power source, waterproof electrical architecture, acoustic coupling, potable-water compatibility, user exposure/noise assessment, cost and demonstrated effectiveness against bathroom scale at low fixture scale.
- Potential fitting insertion: showerhead body / spray face / waterway module.
- Existing Knowledge: TR-046 Piezo / Ultrasonic Fouling Cleaning is already a WATCH / TRANSFER SIGNAL. This discovery therefore extends an existing technology signal rather than creating a new technology record.
- Evidence: IEEE ultrasonic cleaning overview; Agitate Ultrasonics; Puroxi ultrasonic descaler.
- External evidence: https://technav.ieee.org/topic/ultrasonic-cleaning/ ; https://agitateultrasonics.com/ ; https://puroxi.com/products/puroxi-ultrasonic-descaler/

## RS-12 — Idea Convergence

### C-01 — Load-Decoupled Magnetic Handshower Dock
**Lane:** Market Pull + Tech Push / Recombination
**Disposition:** KEEP

- Problem / opportunity: preserve one-handed magnetic docking while reducing failure risk from docking impact, hose load and repeated repositioning.
- Existing solution / source: Moen Magnetix and Delta SureDock magnetic docking; existing Knowledge Sheet CT-013 / ST-017 / TR-050.
- DELTA: magnetic force performs alignment/retention while a separate mechanical load path absorbs docking impact and hose-induced loads; wetted connector/fragile plastic elements are isolated where possible.
- Proposed product response: mechanically captured magnetic dock with controlled impact stop/load spreader and replaceable wear element.
- User benefit: easier docking without sacrificing durability and leakage resistance.
- Mechanism: magnet for alignment + mechanical capture/load path.
- Fitting application: handshower dock / wall supply interface.
- Evidence status: EVIDENCED user problem + commercial precedent; architecture PROPOSED.
- Main uncertainty: exact failure mode/load path of existing competitor products is not fully verified; structural architecture needs testing.
- Suggested downstream prompt: IR-01 → IR-02; if mechanism gap remains, RS-03/RS-04.

### C-02 — Flow-Driven Self-Maintenance Spray Face
**Lane:** Market Pull + Tech Push / Recombination
**Disposition:** WATCH

- Problem / opportunity: recurring mineral clogging and manual cleaning of shower nozzles.
- Existing solution / source: rub-clean silicone nozzles and removable spray faces; industrial fluid-driven rotating cleaning nozzles.
- DELTA / transferable capability: use domestic shower flow itself to create a small controlled movement that mechanically disrupts deposits or promotes drainage, without external power.
- Proposed product response: compact rotating/oscillating maintenance element integrated behind or within the spray face, activated only under a defined flow/pressure condition.
- User benefit: reduced manual cleaning frequency while maintaining spray consistency.
- Mechanism: fluid reaction/turbine torque or controlled hydraulic oscillation.
- Fitting application: spray face / handshower internal waterway.
- Evidence status: industrial mechanism EVIDENCED; shower transfer PROPOSED/UNKNOWN.
- Main uncertainty: domestic pressure/flow may be insufficient after accounting for pressure loss; added moving parts may create scale, noise, leakage and durability risks.
- Suggested downstream prompt: IR-01 → IR-02 → RS-03/RS-04/RS-06.

### C-03 — Passive Dry-Drain Spray Face
**Lane:** Tech Push
**Disposition:** WATCH

- Problem / opportunity: residual water on exposed shower surfaces can dry and leave mineral deposits; repeated cleaning is required.
- Existing solution / source: rub-clean nozzles, removable spray faces, general self-draining concepts.
- DELTA / transferable capability: use engineered capillary/wettability/topography to move residual water away from the nozzle face toward a controlled drain path after flow stops.
- Proposed product response: patterned spray-face surface plus hidden drainage channels that promote post-use water evacuation without moving parts.
- User benefit: less standing water and potentially slower visible scale formation on the spray face.
- Mechanism: directional capillary/wettability gradient.
- Fitting application: exposed spray face / rainhead surface.
- Evidence status: physical principle EVIDENCED; shower durability and scale reduction PROPOSED/UNKNOWN.
- Main uncertainty: soap, cleaners, mineral deposits, finish wear and manufacturing tolerances may degrade the surface effect.
- Suggested downstream prompt: IR-01 → RS-03/RS-06.
