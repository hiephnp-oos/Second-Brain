## RS-10 — Market Pull Scout

### MP-01 — Low-pressure shower performance without adding a pump
**Status:** EVIDENCED market signal; architecture remains PROPOSED.

- WHO: users with low or variable incoming water pressure.
- CONTEXT: Knowledge Sheet MS-001 records weak spray and reduced coverage as an active shower signal.
- NEED / PAIN: maintain useful spray force and coverage without installing an external booster.
- BEST EXISTING SOLUTION: pressure-compensating/adaptive nozzles, air entrainment, booster pumps and engineered spray networks.
- REMAINING LIMITATION: adaptive nozzle and water-shaping solutions are already commercially mature; the remaining opportunity requires a measurable improvement in useful spray performance across a wider pressure range or lower system complexity.
- MEANINGFUL DELTA: maintain defined spray-force/coverage performance across a wider low-pressure range while keeping pressure loss, noise, part count and cost bounded.
- REQUIRED FUNCTION: passively redistribute hydraulic energy and/or adapt outlet resistance according to inlet pressure.
- Candidate architecture seed: multi-resistance spray network coordinated with pressure-responsive outlet behavior. **PROPOSED only.**
- Existing Knowledge collision: TR-001, TR-020 and TR-033 already cover pressure compensation, dual-resistance networks and pressure-responsive nozzles.
- Discovery disposition: WATCH.

### MP-02 — Reduce waiting and wasted cold water before a shower becomes usable
**Status:** EVIDENCED market signal; existing supplier precedent identified.

- WHO: users waiting for hot water at shower start.
- CONTEXT: Knowledge Sheet MS-003 records the desire for faster warm-up with less cold-water discharge.
- BEST EXISTING SOLUTION: hot-water recirculation, local heating/storage and temperature-triggered diversion.
- REMAINING LIMITATION: system-level solutions add infrastructure/energy/complexity; fixture-side solutions need safe temperature detection and a useful drain/pause architecture.
- MEANINGFUL DELTA: reduce waiting and discarded cold water without requiring a large auxiliary system.
- REQUIRED FUNCTION: detect/route initial cold water and automatically transition when target temperature is reached.
- Existing Supplier precedent: ST-054 iDrain / Cold-water Drainage already describes temperature-targeted initial drainage and pause.
- Discovery disposition: DROP unless a materially different architecture or measurable advantage is established.
